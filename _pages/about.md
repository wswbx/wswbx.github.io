Hello! I am **Boxiao Wang (王博霄)**, an incoming direct Ph.D. student at the [Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)](http://www.ia.ac.cn/). I completed my undergraduate study in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics at the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS)](https://www.ucas.ac.cn/) in 2026.

My research sits at the intersection of **LLM agents**, **reinforcement learning**, and **AI for Science**. I am especially interested in self-evolving agents that learn from search and environment feedback, use tools to reason about scientific data, and discover interpretable equations.
